3. Underage players and protection

Adfoot admits players from the age of 12. Every player under 18 must have a consent from a parent or legal guardian recorded by the administration. That consent may be confirmed orally or in writing; the team can explain the process in a language the parent understands. The parent does not need to create an account or sign anything in the application.

The underage player accepts these terms in the application themselves, like any other account holder. That general acceptance does not replace the parental consent recorded by the administration. Until the parental consent is recorded and approved, the minor's record is not shown to recruiters and media are not published.

Once the consent is recorded, a reduced search record for the minor is accessible only to verified recruiters or clubs. It contains only the limited football information needed for search (for example position, nationality, year of birth or club level, where those are filled in); it gives no access to the full profile, the CV or private contact details. Contact requests go through the Adfoot administration, which may review them before passing them on. Recruiters must not bypass that mediation, nor contact the minor directly through another channel using information obtained on the service.

The media permission is separate from the consent to create the record, and videos are moderated before publication. A parent or guardian may contact support@adfoot.org to request the activation, deactivation or withdrawal of the parental consent and the media permission. The profiles and media concerned may be hidden while the request is processed.

Where an age is missing, inconsistent or disputed, Adfoot may provisionally apply the protections intended for minors until it is verified. Anyone may report an age error or a risk concerning a minor to support@adfoot.org.
